# AI Personal Blog

**Modern AI-powered blogging platform**

Built and maintained by **ThidarM**

---

## Problem

Writing and managing a personal blog is harder than it should be.

- Writing good posts takes a lot of time.
- Managing and organizing posts is inconvenient.
- Most personal blogs lack AI-assisted writing tools.

---

## Solution

**AI Personal Blog** makes creating and managing content effortless.

- Helps users create, edit, and manage blog posts from one dashboard.
- **Gemini AI** assists with content generation.
- **Supabase** handles authentication and the database.
- Responsive, modern UI that works on every device.

---

## Main Features

- User authentication
- Create, edit, and delete posts
- AI-assisted content generation
- Image upload support
- Fully responsive design
- Personal dashboard
- Public blog pages for readers
